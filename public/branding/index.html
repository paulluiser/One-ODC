05 / THE COMPANY CONTEXT

Created for
Opulence Design Concept.

A Philippine luxury home and design retailer, Opulence Design Concept curates international furniture, homeware, and kitchen brands. Its official portfolio includes Fornasetti, Versace Home, Jonathan Adler, and Stosa Cucine.

The One ODC identity translates that design context into a calm, architectural workspace for the team behind each project. This is a new internal product identity.
