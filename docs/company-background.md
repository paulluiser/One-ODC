# Opulence Design Concept — brief background research

Research date: 13 October 2026, Asia/Manila.

Opulence Design Concept is a Philippine luxury home and design retailer. Its official catalog brings together furniture, tableware, decorative accessories, and kitchen offerings from international brands, including Fornasetti, Versace Home, Dolce & Gabbana Casa, Jonathan Adler, Polspotten, and Stosa Cucine. Its stated approach emphasizes curated pieces and bringing luxury into the home. [Official catalog](https://opulencedesignconcept.com/).

Manila Bulletin identifies Gerry Sy and Jinky Tobiano Sy as the owners and reports that their first showroom opened on Connecticut Street in Greenhills in 2019. [Manila Bulletin, 8 June 2022](https://mb.com.ph/2022/6/8/bring-a-little-la-dolce-vita-home).

BusinessMirror's January 2026 coverage describes a Greenbelt 5 concept store with spaces dedicated to different design brands. This supports a positioning around curated design experiences, alongside its product range. [BusinessMirror, 3 January 2026](https://businessmirror.com.ph/2026/01/03/opulence-design-concept-celebrates-launch-of-newest-store-at-greenbelt-5/).

The official Stosa page describes configurable kitchens, attention to detail, and ergonomics. That is relevant context for the cabinetry and space-based work in the supplied PRD. [Official Stosa collection](https://opulencedesignconcept.com/pages/stosa-cucine).

## Implications for One ODC — design interpretation

One ODC should feel precise, composed, and aware of physical space. An architectural frame, a calm application canvas, confident typography, and restrained blue accents connect a premium design business with dependable everyday operations. A small warm mineral/brass accent can add character to brand presentations and login artwork.

The product's role is to connect the people who move a project forward. “One” expresses shared visibility and coordination; it does not collapse the independent commercial, design, installation, and payment states defined in the PRD.

## Evidence limits

The public sources establish company and catalog context. The internal workflows, permission matrix, SFY/Stosa/Both choices, and financial rules come from the supplied PRD. No meaning or external brand identity has been inferred for “SFY.” Public brand listings do not by themselves establish exclusivity or distribution rights. The One ODC identity is newly created for the internal product; it does not replace the company's existing corporate identity.
